Privacy Policy

No Advertisements · India · Effective: April 8, 2026
Summary: Velan Spaces does not display advertisements, does not sell your personal data, and does not share your information with third-party marketing companies. Data is used only to operate the app and deliver project management features to authorized users.

1. Who We Are

Velan Spaces ("we", "us", "our") is a project management application developed and operated by Velan Spaces, based in India. The app is designed for interior design and construction project teams including Admins, Managers, Workers, and Clients. For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at: privacy@velanspaces.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide project management services:

  • Account Information: Employee/Client ID and password (hashed) used for authentication.
  • Profile Data: Name and role (Admin, Manager, Worker, Client) assigned by your organization.
  • Project Data: Project names, timelines, budget information, task updates, design documents, and room details you create or upload within the app.
  • Uploaded Media: Photos, videos, and PDF documents you upload to project rooms (stored securely on Firebase Storage).
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and app version collected automatically for crash diagnostics and compatibility.
  • Usage Data: Screens visited and features used within the app, used only for improving the product experience.

We do not collect: precise GPS location, contacts, call logs, financial account numbers, or any biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and authorize your access to the correct role-based dashboard.
  • To store and display project data, updates, media, and budgets to authorized team members.
  • To send in-app notifications about project updates (no SMS or email marketing).
  • To diagnose technical errors and improve app stability.
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations.

4. Data Storage and Firebase

Velan Spaces uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage) to store and process data. Firebase servers may be located in data centres outside India; by using this app, you consent to this transfer in accordance with applicable law. Firebase is compliant with international data security standards including ISO 27001 and SOC 2.

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

5. No Advertisements

Velan Spaces contains no advertisements of any kind — no banner ads, no video ads, no sponsored content, and no third-party ad networks. Your usage data is never sold to advertisers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within Your Organization: Project data is visible to other authorized users within your organization's project (e.g., Managers can view project budgets; Clients can view project progress).
  • Service Providers: Google Firebase (authentication and storage) — bound by Google's data processing terms.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by Indian courts, law enforcement, or regulatory authorities.

7. Data Retention

Your project data is retained as long as your organization's account is active on Velan Spaces. Upon account deletion or termination, we will delete or anonymize all associated personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Velan Spaces is intended for professional use by adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from users under 18 years of age. If we learn that a minor has provided data, we will promptly delete it.

9. Your Rights (under Indian IT Act and DPDP Act 2023)

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@velanspaces.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted storage (Firebase Secure Storage), role-based access control, TLS encryption in transit, and session token management via Flutter Secure Storage. No system is 100% secure; however, we take all reasonable steps to protect your data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated through the app. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page will reflect the most recent update. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
